FAQ

Have questions?

Who does this model work best for?

Anyone who values clear, conflict-free advice and prefers to keep control of their own investments. It works especially well for proactive DIYers looking for an expert sounding board to optimize complex tax strategies, military or union benefits, and overall financial plans, without paying ongoing asset management fees.

What do "fee-only" and "advice-only" mean?

"Fee-only" means I am paid exclusively by you, my client, never through sales commissions, referral fees, or kickbacks from financial products. "Advice-only" means you pay for expert financial and tax guidance, not for investment management. You keep full control of your accounts, avoid ongoing assets under management (AUM) fees, and receive 100% conflict-free advice.

Why work with an IRS Enrolled Agent (EA) instead of a regular advisor?

An Enrolled Agent is the highest credential awarded by the IRS and signifies technical expertise in tax law. Taxes touch nearly every financial decision, from military retirement transitions and Roth conversions to airline pilot pay structures. Working with an EA means your plan has proactive, year-round tax optimization built in at every step rather than treated as an afterthought. And I can give tax advice, so you won't hear "you'll need to check with your CPA or tax advisor."

Do I have to be a pilot, service member, or veteran to work with you?

Not at all. While I specialize in the tax rules, pension options, and benefit structures specific to active-duty military, veterans, and commercial airline pilots, I welcome any proactive individual or household seeking objective, fee-only financial direction.

What's the difference between a one-time plan, a project, and hourly work?
  • Comprehensive one-time plan: a complete, top-to-bottom roadmap covering cash flow, taxes, investments, insurance, and estate planning (typically 3–6 meetings).
  • Tailored project: focused on a specific, complex issue such as a military-to-civilian career transition, pilot pension evaluation, or major tax strategy session.
  • Hourly consulting: flexible, targeted advice for specific questions when you need an expert sounding board without a broader project scope.
How do pre-meeting videos work, and why do you use them?

To respect your time and keep our live meetings focused on strategy, I record short, personalized video walkthroughs and written breakdowns ahead of our sessions. You review them on your own schedule, process the details, and bring your specific questions to our meeting, so our live time is spent making decisions, not sitting through slides. See demos of the software I use.

What happens after my plan is finished?

Every project and comprehensive plan includes one hour of dedicated follow-up work directed by you within 60 days at no additional cost. As you implement your roadmap, you can submit questions ahead of time, receive a follow-up video analysis, and/or meet with me to troubleshoot real-world execution and confirm you're on track.

Why call it Vector Check Financial Planning?

In aviation and military parlance, a vector check is a quick, proactive alignment to confirm you are on the correct heading before wasting time or fuel in the wrong direction. It reflects how I work with you: I'm not here to take over your controls or force you into ongoing asset management. I provide precise, conflict-free course corrections and tax strategies to keep you on track.

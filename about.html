My story

From DIYer to planner

My journey into financial planning started from personal experience. For years, I managed my own finances as a DIYer while I served in the Air Force. But as life grew more complex, I realized I wanted professional, objective guidance to make sure I wasn't missing anything.

When I searched for a planner, I ran into a major obstacle: almost every advisor wanted to take over managing my investments and charge a percentage of my portfolio. I didn't want to hand over my assets. I just wanted expert tax and financial advice on a flexible, transparent basis. When I couldn't find a firm with a model that fit my needs, I decided to build one.

The mission

Make conflict-free, high-value financial planning accessible.

Whether you're navigating complex military benefits, airline pilot compensation, or intricate tax strategies, I'm here to give you straightforward one-time plans, projects, or hourly planning so you remain in complete control of your financial future.

As a Fee-Only fiduciary and IRS Enrolled Agent, I specialize in serving military members and airline pilots, but I'm proud to work with clients from all walks of life.

The name

Why "Vector Check"?

A vector check, originally an aviation operational term that spread into military slang, means verifying that a project, strategy, or person is moving in the correct direction before too much time or energy is spent on the wrong heading.

I chose this name because it reflects exactly how I'll work with you: I'm not here to take over your controls or force you into ongoing asset management. I provide the precise, conflict-free course corrections and tax strategies you need to stay on track, giving you complete confidence and control over your financial flight path.
